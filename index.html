Fire Safety Knowledge & Compliance FAQ

Understanding statutory fire safety obligations is essential for every commercial establishment, office building, and warehouse in New Delhi.

Explore answers to common questions regarding equipment upkeep, legal mandates, and safety protocols.

How often must fire extinguishers be inspected and refilled? -
Fire extinguishers require a visual inspection every month and a certified professional inspection annually. Refilling is mandatory immediately after any use, or as per manufacturer guidelines (typically every 3 to 5 years depending on the agent type like ABC Powder or CO2).
What services are included under your Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)? +
Our comprehensive AMC covers regular scheduled visits, pressure testing of extinguishers, operational testing of fire alarm control panels, valve lubrication for hydrants, minor repairs, and issuance of compliance verification logs for your records.
